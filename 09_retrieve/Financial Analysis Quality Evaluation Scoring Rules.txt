1. Evaluation System Overview
================================================================================

This evaluation system employs a multi-dimensional weighted scoring mechanism 
to comprehensively assess the quality of financial analysis reports. The 
evaluation is rule-based and automated, without relying on external tools.

Evaluation Targets:
  - Company Analysis Reports
  - Industry Analysis Reports

Scoring Range: 0-100 points
Output Format: Detailed dimension scores + Overall score + Improvement suggestions

================================================================================
2. Six Evaluation Dimensions and Weights
================================================================================

Dimension                  Weight    Max Score    Description
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Completeness                20%        100        All required fields present
Traceability                25%        100        URL citation coverage & quality
Consistency                 20%        100        Score logical consistency
Depth                       15%        100        Analysis detail & insightfulness
Timeliness                  10%        100        Data recency
Balance                     10%        100        Positive/negative balance

Overall Score Formula:
  Overall Score = Completeness × 0.20 
                + Traceability × 0.25 
                + Consistency × 0.20 
                + Depth × 0.15 
                + Timeliness × 0.10 
                + Balance × 0.10

3. Completeness Scoring Rules - 20%
================================================================================

Base Score: 100 points
Deduction Mechanism: Cumulative deductions for missing items
Minimum Score: 0 points (no negative scores)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Basic Fields Check (5 points deducted per missing field)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Required Fields:
  ✓ analysis_date         - Analysis date
  ✓ analysis_period       - Analysis period
  ✓ total_news_analyzed   - Total number of news analyzed

Deduction Examples:
  - Missing analysis_date: deduct 5 points
  - Missing analysis_period and total_news_analyzed: deduct 10 points

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 Storyline Completeness Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Storyline Required Fields:
  ✓ summary          - Summary (200-400 characters)
  ✓ key_events       - Key events list
  ✓ timeline         - Timeline description
  ✓ source_urls      - Source URLs list

Deduction Rules:
  - Completely missing storyline field: deduct 15 points
  - Each missing sub-field within storyline: deduct 5 points

Examples:
  - Missing storyline.summary: deduct 5 points
  - Missing storyline.key_events and timeline: deduct 10 points

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 Analysis Dimension Completeness Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Company Analysis] Required Dimensions (6):
  1. financial_impact      - Financial impact
  2. operational_impact    - Operational impact
  3. market_impact         - Market impact
  4. regulatory_impact     - Regulatory impact
  5. strategic_impact      - Strategic impact
  6. future_outlook        - Future outlook

[Industry Analysis] Required Dimensions (6):
  1. industry_trend            - Industry trend
  2. competitive_landscape     - Competitive landscape
  3. policy_regulatory         - Policy & regulatory
  4. supply_chain_ecosystem    - Supply chain ecosystem
  5. investment_attractiveness - Investment attractiveness
  6. future_outlook            - Future outlook

Required Fields for Each Dimension:
  ✓ score        - Score (integer, -10 to 10)
  ✓ analysis     - Analysis text (100-200 words)
  ✓ source_urls  - Source URLs list (at least 2-3)

Deduction Rules:
  - Completely missing one dimension: deduct 10 points
  - Each missing required field within dimension: deduct 3 points

Examples:
  - Missing financial_impact dimension: deduct 10 points
  - financial_impact missing score and source_urls: deduct 6 points

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4 Overall Assessment Completeness Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Required Fields:
  ✓ total_score    - Total score (sum of all dimension scores)
  ✓ recommendation - Recommendation (Buy/Hold/Sell, etc.)
  ✓ confidence     - Confidence (0-1 float)
  ✓ summary        - Overall summary
  ✓ key_insights   - Key insights list (at least 3)

Deduction Rules:
  - Each missing field: deduct 3 points

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.5 Improvement Suggestion Trigger Conditions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Trigger Condition:
  - If present dimensions < 4

Generated Suggestion:
  "Recommend including at least 4+ analysis dimensions"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.6 Output Metrics
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Return Data Structure:
  {
    'score': 85,                      # Final score
    'max_score': 100,                 # Maximum score
    'present_dimensions': 6,          # Actual dimensions present
    'expected_dimensions': 6,         # Expected dimensions
    'issues': [...],                  # Issues list
    'recommendations': [...]          # Recommendations list
  }

4. Traceability Scoring Rules - 25%
================================================================================

Base Score: 100 points
Deduction Mechanism: Cumulative deductions based on URL coverage
Minimum Score: 0 points

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 URL Coverage Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Company Analysis] Dimensions Checked (8):
  storyline, financial_impact, operational_impact, market_impact,
  regulatory_impact, strategic_impact, future_outlook, overall_assessment

[Industry Analysis] Dimensions Checked (8):
  storyline, industry_trend, competitive_landscape, policy_regulatory,
  supply_chain_ecosystem, investment_attractiveness, future_outlook, 
  overall_assessment

Deduction Rules:
  - Dimension missing source_urls field or empty list: deduct 10 points per dimension
  - URL count < 2: deduct 5 points (recommend at least 2-3)

Examples:
  - financial_impact has no source_urls: deduct 10 points
  - market_impact has only 1 URL: deduct 5 points

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 URL Coverage Rate Calculation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Formula:
  Coverage Rate = (Dimensions with URLs / Total Dimensions) × 100%

Improvement Suggestion Trigger Conditions:
  - Coverage < 80%: Generate suggestion "URL coverage is only X%, target should be >80%"
  - Total URLs < 10: Generate suggestion "Total URL count (X) is low, recommend at least 15-20"

Note: Low coverage generates suggestions but does not directly deduct points

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 Text Citation Annotation Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Check Content:
  Whether each dimension's analysis text contains citation annotations
  
Valid Annotation Formats:
  - [参考来源: URL]
  - [Source: URL]

Deduction Rules:
  - If less than 50% of dimensions have text citation annotations: deduct 10 points

Examples:
  - Only 3 out of 8 dimensions have [Source: URL] annotations: deduct 10 points
  - 5 out of 8 dimensions have annotations: no deduction

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.4 Output Metrics
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Return Data Structure:
  {
    'score': 90,                          # Final score
    'max_score': 100,                     # Maximum score
    'url_coverage_rate': 87.5,            # URL coverage rate (%)
    'dimensions_with_urls': 7,            # Dimensions with URLs
    'total_dimensions': 8,                # Total dimensions
    'total_urls': 24,                     # Total URLs
    'avg_urls_per_dimension': 3.0,        # Average URLs per dimension
    'url_citations_in_text': 6,           # Citations in text
    'issues': [...],                      # Issues list
    'recommendations': [...]              # Recommendations list
  }

5. Consistency Scoring Rules - 20%
================================================================================

Base Score: 100 points
Deduction Mechanism: Cumulative deductions for logical inconsistencies
Minimum Score: 0 points

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Dimension Score Range Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Valid Range: -10 to +10 (inclusive)

Deduction Rules:
  - Score is not numeric type (int/float): deduct 10 points
  - Score < -10 or > 10: deduct 10 points

Examples:
  - financial_impact.score = "high" (string): deduct 10 points
  - market_impact.score = 15 (out of range): deduct 10 points
  - operational_impact.score = -5 (valid): no deduction

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2 Total Score Consistency Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Calculate Expected Total:
  Expected Total = Sum(All dimension scores)

Tolerance Calculation:
  Tolerance = max(|Expected Total| × 0.15, 10)

Validation Rule:
  |total_score - expected_total| ≤ tolerance

Deduction Rules:
  - Difference exceeds tolerance: deduct 15 points
  - total_score outside reasonable range [-num_dims×10, num_dims×10]: deduct 10 points

Example 1 (Pass):
  - 6 dimension scores: 2, 1, 3, 0, 1, 0
  - Expected Total = 7
  - Tolerance = max(7 × 0.15, 10) = max(1.05, 10) = 10
  - If total_score = 8, then |8 - 7| = 1 < 10 ✓ Pass

Example 2 (Fail):
  - 6 dimension scores: 2, 1, 3, 0, 1, 0
  - Expected Total = 7
  - Tolerance = 10
  - If total_score = 25, then |25 - 7| = 18 > 10 ✗ Deduct 15 points

Example 3 (Range Check):
  - 6 dimensions, reasonable range: [-60, 60]
  - If total_score = 70, out of range ✗ Deduct 10 points

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.3 Confidence Range Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Valid Range: 0 to 1 (inclusive)

Deduction Rules:
  - Not numeric type: deduct 5 points
  - < 0 or > 1: deduct 10 points
  - < 0.6: Generate suggestion (no deduction) "Low confidence (X.XX), recommend re-analysis"

Examples:
  - confidence = 0.85: ✓ Valid
  - confidence = 1.5: ✗ Deduct 10 points
  - confidence = 0.45: Generate suggestion, no deduction

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.4 Recommendation Validity Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Valid Recommendation Values (case-insensitive):

Chinese Recommendations:
  - 买入 (Buy)
  - 持有 (Hold)
  - 卖出 (Sell)
  - 超配 (Overweight)
  - 标配 (Equal-weight)
  - 低配 (Underweight)

English Recommendations:
  - buy
  - hold
  - sell
  - overweight
  - equal-weight
  - underweight

Deduction Rules:
  - Recommendation not in valid list: deduct 5 points

Examples:
  - recommendation = "Buy": ✓ Valid
  - recommendation = "Strong Buy": ✗ Deduct 5 points

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.5 Output Metrics
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Return Data Structure:
  {
    'score': 95,                        # Final score
    'max_score': 100,                   # Maximum score
    'dimension_scores': [2, 1, 3, 0, 1, 0],  # Dimension scores
    'dimension_scores_sum': 7,          # Sum of dimension scores
    'issues': [...],                    # Issues list
    'recommendations': [...]            # Recommendations list
  }

6. Depth Scoring Rules - 15%
================================================================================

Base Score: 100 points
Deduction Mechanism: Cumulative deductions for insufficient depth
Minimum Score: 0 points

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Storyline Detail Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Storyline Summary Length Check:
  - < 100 characters: deduct 10 points
  - 100-200 characters: ✓ Acceptable
  - 200-400 characters: ✓ Ideal
  - > 500 characters: Generate suggestion (no deduction) "Storyline summary is long, consider condensing to 200-400 characters"

Key Events Count Check:
  - < 2 events: deduct 10 points
  - 2-3 events: ✓ Minimum requirement
  - 3-5 events: ✓ Ideal
  - > 5 events: ✓ Excellent

Examples:
  - summary = "Brief" (20 characters): deduct 10 points
  - key_events has only 1 event: deduct 10 points

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.2 Dimension Analysis Word Count Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Company Analysis] Dimensions Checked:
  financial_impact, operational_impact, market_impact,
  regulatory_impact, strategic_impact, future_outlook

[Industry Analysis] Dimensions Checked:
  industry_trend, competitive_landscape, policy_regulatory,
  supply_chain_ecosystem, investment_attractiveness, future_outlook

Check Standard:
  - analysis text length < 50 characters: deduct 5 points per dimension

Improvement Suggestion:
  - If dimensions have short analyses: Generate suggestion "Each dimension analysis should be at least 100-200 words"

Examples:
  - financial_impact.analysis only 30 characters: deduct 5 points
  - 3 dimensions with short analyses: deduct 15 points

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.3 Key Insights Count Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Check Location: overall_assessment.key_insights

Count Requirements:
  - < 3 insights: deduct 10 points
  - 3-5 insights: ✓ Ideal
  - > 5 insights: ✓ Excellent

Examples:
  - key_insights has only 2: deduct 10 points
  - key_insights has 4: ✓ Valid

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.4 Future Outlook Phase Analysis Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Required Phase Analysis Fields:
  ✓ short_term_impact   - Short-term impact (1-3 months)
  ✓ medium_term_impact  - Medium-term impact (3-12 months)
  ✓ long_term_impact    - Long-term impact (1-3 years)

Deduction Rules:
  - Each missing phase analysis: deduct 5 points

Examples:
  - Missing short_term_impact and long_term_impact: deduct 10 points
  - All three phases present: ✓ Valid

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.5 Output Metrics
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Return Data Structure:
  {
    'score': 80,                        # Final score
    'max_score': 100,                   # Maximum score
    'storyline_summary_length': 250,    # Storyline summary length
    'key_events_count': 4,              # Key events count
    'key_insights_count': 3,            # Key insights count
    'issues': [...],                    # Issues list
    'recommendations': [...]            # Recommendations list
  }

7. Timeliness Scoring Rules - 10%
================================================================================

Base Score: 100 points
Deduction Mechanism: Deductions based on data staleness
Minimum Score: 0 points

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Analysis Date Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Date Format Requirement: YYYY-MM-DD

Deduction Rules:
  - Invalid date format: deduct 10 points
  - More than 7 days old: deduct 20 points
  - 4-7 days old: Generate suggestion (no deduction) "Analysis date is YYYY-MM-DD, recommend update"
  - ≤ 3 days old: ✓ Valid

Examples:
  - analysis_date = "2026-04-20" (7 days ago): deduct 20 points
  - analysis_date = "2026-04-25" (2 days ago): ✓ Valid
  - analysis_date = "invalid": deduct 10 points

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.2 Analysis Period Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Extract days from analysis_period field (e.g., "Past 7 days" extracts 7)

Recommended Range: 7-90 days

Improvement Suggestion Trigger Conditions:
  - < 3 days: Generate suggestion "Analysis period is short (X days), recommend 7-30 days"
  - > 90 days: Generate suggestion "Analysis period is long (X days), may contain outdated information"
  - 3-7 days: ✓ Acceptable
  - 7-90 days: ✓ Ideal

Note: Inappropriate analysis period only generates suggestions, no direct deduction

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.3 Output Metrics
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Return Data Structure:
  {
    'score': 100,                       # Final score
    'max_score': 100,                   # Maximum score
    'analysis_date': '2026-04-27',      # Analysis date
    'issues': [...],                    # Issues list
    'recommendations': [...]            # Recommendations list
  }

8. Balance Scoring Rules - 10%
================================================================================

Base Score: 100 points
Deduction Mechanism: Deductions based on analysis bias
Minimum Score: 0 points

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.1 Positive/Negative Score Distribution Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Company Analysis] Dimensions Checked:
  financial_impact, operational_impact, market_impact,
  regulatory_impact, strategic_impact, future_outlook

[Industry Analysis] Dimensions Checked:
  industry_trend, competitive_landscape, policy_regulatory,
  supply_chain_ecosystem, investment_attractiveness, future_outlook

Score Classification:
  - Positive: score > 2
  - Neutral: -2 ≤ score ≤ 2
  - Negative: score < -2

Calculate Ratios:
  positive_ratio = positive_count / total_scored
  negative_ratio = negative_count / total_scored

Deduction Rules:
  - positive_ratio > 0.8 (too positive): deduct 10 points
  - negative_ratio > 0.8 (too negative): deduct 10 points

Example 1 (Too Positive):
  - 6 dimension scores: 5, 7, 3, 2, 6, 4
  - positive_count = 5, neutral_count = 1, negative_count = 0
  - positive_ratio = 5/6 = 0.83 > 0.8
  - Deduct 10 points, generate suggestion "Analysis is too positive, recommend adding risk warnings"

Example 2 (Balanced):
  - 6 dimension scores: 2, -1, 3, 0, -2, 1
  - positive_count = 2, neutral_count = 3, negative_count = 1
  - positive_ratio = 2/6 = 0.33, negative_ratio = 1/6 = 0.17
  - ✓ Valid, no deduction

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.2 Risk Analysis Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Check Location: future_outlook.risk_analysis

Length Requirement: ≥ 30 characters

Deduction Rules:
  - Missing risk_analysis field or length < 30: deduct 10 points

Improvement Suggestion:
  - If risk analysis is insufficient: Generate suggestion "Missing detailed risk analysis"

Examples:
  - risk_analysis = "" (empty string): deduct 10 points
  - risk_analysis = "Market risks exist" (20 characters): deduct 10 points
  - risk_analysis = "Market volatility risks, policy change risks..." (>30 characters): ✓ Valid

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.3 Output Metrics
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Return Data Structure:
  {
    'score': 90,                        # Final score
    'max_score': 100,                   # Maximum score
    'positive_dimensions': 2,           # Positive dimensions
    'negative_dimensions': 1,           # Negative dimensions
    'neutral_dimensions': 3,            # Neutral dimensions
    'issues': [...],                    # Issues list
    'recommendations': [...]            # Recommendations list
  }

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

10. Scoring Calculation Examples
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Example 1: High-Quality Company Analysis Report
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Assumed Evaluation Results:
  - Completeness:  95 points (missing 1 small field)
  - Traceability:  90 points (URL coverage 87.5%)
  - Consistency:   100 points (fully consistent)
  - Depth:         85 points (some analyses slightly short)
  - Timeliness:    100 points (analyzed today)
  - Balance:       90 points (slight bias but has risk analysis)

Overall Score Calculation:
  Overall = 95 × 0.20 + 90 × 0.25 + 100 × 0.20 + 85 × 0.15 + 100 × 0.10 + 90 × 0.10
          = 19.0 + 22.5 + 20.0 + 12.75 + 10.0 + 9.0
          = 93.25 points

Grade: A (Excellent)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Example 2: Medium-Quality Industry Analysis Report
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Assumed Evaluation Results:
  - Completeness:  75 points (missing 2 dimensions)
  - Traceability:  70 points (URL coverage 62.5%)
  - Consistency:   85 points (total_score slightly off)
  - Depth:         80 points (storyline slightly short)
  - Timeliness:    80 points (5-day-old analysis)
  - Balance:       100 points (good balance)

Overall Score Calculation:
  Overall = 75 × 0.20 + 70 × 0.25 + 85 × 0.20 + 80 × 0.15 + 80 × 0.10 + 100 × 0.10
          = 15.0 + 17.5 + 17.0 + 12.0 + 8.0 + 10.0
          = 79.5 points

Grade: C (Average)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Example 3: Low-Quality Analysis Report
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Assumed Evaluation Results:
  - Completeness:  50 points (missing multiple dimensions and fields)
  - Traceability:  40 points (URL coverage 37.5%)
  - Consistency:   60 points (score inconsistency)
  - Depth:         55 points (all analyses very short)
  - Timeliness:    80 points (5-day-old analysis)
  - Balance:       70 points (too positive)

Overall Score Calculation:
  Overall = 50 × 0.20 + 40 × 0.25 + 60 × 0.20 + 55 × 0.15 + 80 × 0.10 + 70 × 0.10
          = 10.0 + 10.0 + 12.0 + 8.25 + 8.0 + 7.0
          = 55.25 points

Grade: F (Fail)

Appendix A: Common Issues and Solutions
================================================================================

Issue 1: Low Completeness Score
  Cause: Missing required fields or dimensions
  Solution: Ensure all required fields and dimensions are present, each dimension 
            includes score, analysis, source_urls

Issue 2: Low Traceability Score
  Cause: Insufficient URLs or missing citation annotations
  Solution: Provide at least 2-3 URLs per dimension, use [Source: URL] annotations 
            in analysis text

Issue 3: Low Consistency Score
  Cause: total_score inconsistent with sum of dimension scores
  Solution: Ensure total_score = sum(all dimension scores), allow 15% or 10 point 
            tolerance

Issue 4: Low Depth Score
  Cause: Analysis text too short or missing key content
  Solution: Each dimension analysis at least 100-200 words, storyline summary 
            200-400 characters, provide 3-5 key_insights

Issue 5: Low Timeliness Score
  Cause: Analysis date too old
  Solution: Use latest news data for analysis, ensure analysis date within 3 days

Issue 6: Low Balance Score
  Cause: Analysis too biased toward positive or negative
  Solution: Objectively analyze both sides, provide detailed risk analysis 
            (at least 30 characters)

Appendix C: Dimension-Specific Scoring Guidelines (-10 to +10 Scale)
================================================================================

Positive Scores (Beneficial Impact):
  +9 to +10: Exceptional positive impact - transformative growth, breakthrough 
             achievements, major competitive advantages
  +7 to +8:  Strong positive impact - significant revenue growth, market 
             expansion, strategic wins
  +5 to +6:  Moderate positive impact - steady improvement, incremental gains, 
             favorable trends
  +3 to +4:  Mild positive impact - slight improvements, minor benefits, 
             small opportunities
  +1 to +2:  Minimal positive impact - negligible benefits, marginal improvements

Neutral Score:
  0:         No significant impact or mixed effects that cancel out

Negative Scores (Detrimental Impact):
  -1 to -2:  Minimal negative impact - slight concerns, minor setbacks
  -3 to -4:  Mild negative impact - noticeable challenges, small losses, 
             emerging risks
  -5 to -6:  Moderate negative impact - significant headwinds, measurable 
             losses, concerning trends
  -7 to -8:  Strong negative impact - serious problems, substantial losses, 
             major threats
  -9 to -10: Exceptional negative impact - crisis situation, catastrophic 
             losses, existential threats
